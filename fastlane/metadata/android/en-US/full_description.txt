YingXia Player is a 4.16 MB, open-source Android local video player with zero internet permissions and a real AES-256-GCM streaming encrypted video vault.

No INTERNET or ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions, no ads, no accounts, and no trackers — everything stays strictly offline on your device.

Encrypted Video Vault:
- Encrypts videos with Google Tink AES256_GCM_HKDF_1MB Streaming AEAD and PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (600,000 iterations), plus optional hardware-backed Biometric (fingerprint) quick unlock.
- Streams and decrypts chunks in memory with smooth seeking — zero plaintext video caches or thumbnails written to disk.
- Verifies full ciphertext integrity with SHA-256 before requesting Android's system dialog to remove gallery originals.
- Supports uninstall-safe pure-ciphertext cold backups to local storage or USB OTG drives, plus offline Keystore-backed recovery questions.
- Protects vault playback with FLAG_SECURE, instant lock on backgrounding, and a one-tap Lock button during playback.
- Share directly from your system gallery to Watch without history or encrypt straight into the vault without granting full library access.

Playback & Local Library Tools:
- 8 playback speeds (0.25x–3x) plus 5 selectable press-and-hold boost speeds (1.5x / 2x / 2.5x / 3x / 4x) with live horizontal slide-to-switch while holding.
- A–B segment loop, timestamped bookmarks with notes, sleep timer, double-tap pause, swipe seek, fullscreen brightness/volume gestures, pinch-to-zoom, crop black bars, and touch guard.
- Minimalist 2.0 UI with 2-column library grid, pinyin initial search, SHA-256 verified folder moves, offline duplicate & corrupt file checker, password-encrypted local backups (.yxbackup.enc), and English / Simplified Chinese switching.
