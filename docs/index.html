Real Streaming AEAD Vault — Zero Plaintext Cache

Powered by Google Tink AES256_GCM_HKDF_1MB and PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (600,000 iterations). Engineered so private videos are verified before originals are removed.

1

Encrypt & SHA-256 Verify

Writes 1 MB authenticated ciphertext chunks into app-private noBackupFilesDir and verifies SHA-256 end to end.

2

User-Confirmed Removal

Requests gallery deletion via Android's system dialog only after verification succeeds. Cancelling keeps the original.

3

In-Memory Stream Decrypt

Custom Media3 VaultDataSource decrypts segments on the fly with smooth random seeking — zero plaintext temp files.

4

One-Tap Lock & Zero-Perm Share

Tap Lock during playback to stop & return home instantly. Share from your gallery to encrypt without granting full library access.
